Privacy Policy – AI Agent

Last updated: 10 November 2026

"AI Agent" is a private, internal application operated by Slabbert Chartered Accountants, 2489 Church St, North Gower, ON K0A 2T0, Canada ("we"). It connects our own AI assistant to QuickBooks Online so that authorised members of our firm can look up and update accounting records by chat. It is not offered to the public.

What data we access

With the permission of a QuickBooks administrator, the app accesses accounting data in the connected QuickBooks Online company: for example customers, vendors, invoices, bills, payments, accounts and reports. We do not access payment card or bank login details.

How we use it

Data is used only to answer questions and carry out tasks requested by authorised users of our firm. Changes to QuickBooks are made only after an authorised user explicitly approves them. The app never deletes records. Every change is logged.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent data, and we do not share it with third parties except the service providers needed to run the assistant (hosting, and the AI model provider that processes requests), who are bound to keep it confidential. We may disclose data where required by law.

Storage and security

Access credentials are stored encrypted-at-rest or with restricted file permissions on our own servers and are never shared. Access can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the app in QuickBooks.

Retention

We keep only what is needed to provide the service. QuickBooks remains the system of record; the app does not keep its own copy of your books beyond short-term processing and the change log.

Your rights

We handle personal information in line with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). To ask about, access or correct personal information, contact us below.
